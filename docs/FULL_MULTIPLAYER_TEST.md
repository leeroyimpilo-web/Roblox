# Full Multiplayer Test

Run this after Rojo sync using Roblox Studio with at least two local players.

## Boot

1. Start two players.
2. Confirm no red startup errors.
3. Confirm each player receives a different floating Core island.
4. Confirm both players spawn on their own islands.
5. Confirm the central raid hub exists.

## Tutorial

6. Claim Core Charge.
7. Confirm tutorial advances and the +500 Energy tutorial bonus arrives.
8. Upgrade the Core once.
9. Confirm tutorial advances to Steal a Core.
10. Cross to the other player's island.
11. Steal their Core fragment.
12. Confirm the thief carries a bright visible fragment.
13. Confirm the victim receives an alert.
14. Bank the fragment at the thief's green pad.
15. Confirm tutorial completion reward.

## Recovery

16. Perform another theft.
17. Have the victim use RECOVER CORE on the carried fragment.
18. Confirm stolen Charge is not permanently lost.
19. Confirm thief drops the fragment on death.

## Revenge / Wanted

20. Complete enough consecutive raids to trigger WANTED.
21. Confirm wanted visual and bounty.
22. Confirm victim receives a revenge target.
23. Complete a revenge heist and confirm payout bonus.
24. Claim a wanted bounty and confirm streak resets.

## Defenses

25. Upgrade Pulse Trap.
26. Have another player cross the trap zone.
27. Confirm movement slows temporarily.
28. Confirm trap cooldown prevents constant retriggering.

## Skins

29. Reach Core Level 5 and equip Solar Gold.
30. Confirm the physical Core changes colour.
31. Verify locked skins cannot be equipped.

## Mega Core

32. Add your Roblox UserId to AdminUserIds.
33. Run /megacore 60.
34. Confirm server-wide event banner.
35. Drain the Mega Core.
36. Confirm Energy and weekly points.
37. Confirm per-player drain cooldown.
38. Confirm event champion reward.

## Core Meltdown

39. Run /meltdown 60.
40. Confirm personal Cores generate faster.
41. Confirm HUD displays Core event multiplier.

## Leaderboards

42. Complete raids and Mega Core drains.
43. Confirm server raid leaderboard updates.
44. Confirm richest Core ranking updates.
45. In a published test experience, confirm global/weekly ordered boards populate.

## Adventure systems

46. Use the Adventure Worlds portal.
47. Test Energy nodes and Power upgrades.
48. Hatch/equip companions.
49. Unlock Jungle through Space.
50. Defeat Jungle Titan.
51. Rebirth.
52. Confirm retained Core/pet progression behaves as intended.

## Social systems

53. Create a party.
54. Verify party Energy multiplier.
55. Gift Energy.
56. Trade one companion each.
57. Cancel a second trade and verify ownership remains unchanged.

## Retention

58. Claim daily reward twice; second attempt must fail.
59. Redeem LAUNCH twice; second attempt must fail.
60. Leave and rejoin; verify Core Level, Charge, skins, trap, pets, currencies and stats persist.

## Monetization

After publishing and inserting real IDs:
61. Test each Game Pass.
62. Test each Developer Product.
63. Test subscription status.
64. Cancel purchase prompts and verify no reward.
65. Rejoin after purchases and verify entitlement/reward persistence.

## Mobile

66. Test on an Android phone.
67. Check HUD safe areas, modal scrolling and ProximityPrompts.
68. Test stealing, recovery and banking with touch controls.
